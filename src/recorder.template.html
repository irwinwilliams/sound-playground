How it works
  1. Pick any clip below (or go in order with Next) and record it. A new take replaces the old one. Silence at the ends is trimmed and volume is evened out.
  2. Hold the stretchy sounds (a e i o u m n s f l r v sh th) steady for about 1½ seconds. Say the quick sounds short, with no “uh”.
  3. Your clips are kept in this browser, so you can stop and come back. When you’re ready, click Save voice pack.
  4. In the game, hold Grown-ups, open Voice studio, click Import voice pack and choose that file.
